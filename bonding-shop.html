Local bonding option, at the shop (no VPS)

No cloud server, no tunnel; everything stays on the shop PC.

Speed: Up to 5x one link in theory; realistically 60-80% of that combined, when traffic can use several lines.

How it really behaves

  • Without a VPS, true single-connection bonding via MPTCP only works when the far-end server also speaks MPTCP. Most websites don't.
  • Otherwise the PC load-balances separate connections across the lines: many users and downloads together get the combined speed, while one single download or stream stays on one line.
  • Services at the shop can't be reached from the internet without a VPS or tunnel.

Need one true merged line or remote access?

A VPS merges all five lines into one connection with one public IP.
